PROCESS

Your part in four steps.

  1. 1

    Send photos and sizes

    Share a few photos of the part, the make and model of the device and any measurements you know.

  2. 2

    We model it and quote

    We model the part to size in Bambu Studio and send you the price and timing.

  3. 3

    Test print

    For parts that must fit precisely, we test-print first and check the fit.

  4. 4

    We print and ship

    We print the approved part on our X2D, check it by hand and ship it.

TIPS

Good measurements, a part that fits first time.

  • Photograph the part next to a ruler or tape, from the top and the side.
  • If you have calipers, note hole diameters, wall thickness and overall size.
  • Keep every piece of the broken part; fitting them together helps a lot.
  • Add a photo of the model label on the back or underside of the device.
  • If needed, we may ask you to send us the broken part or its twin.

FAQ

How is the price set?

By size, complexity and quantity. We give a firm price once we see the photos and sizes, and we don’t start without your approval.

How long does it take?

We include design and print time in the quote. Simple parts are much quicker.

Will it be identical to the original?

We design it to match the original’s size and function. Colour and surface texture may differ.

What material do you use?

PLA as standard. We check together whether it suits where the part will be used (heat, load).
